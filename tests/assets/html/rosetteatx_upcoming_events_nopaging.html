ATX Music Series: Guy Forsyth Trio

  • Thursday, February 19, 2026
  • 8:00 PM 9:30 PM
  • The Rosette @ Austin Classical Guitar (map)
  • Google Calendar ICS

We are ecstatic to present the Guy Forsyth Trio at The Rosette as part of our ATX Music Series. Founder of iconic Austin sonic experiences like Asylum Street Spankers and (now) The Hot Nut Riveters, Forsyth has played around the world, made over 20 records, won many awards, and helped define Austin music. He's a one-of-a-kind Austin original. He not only writes and sings his own songs, but he enjoys telling the stories behind their inspiration. He is a modern American Songster. Not one song is the same, but they share the same care and craftsmanship. No matter the tune, no matter the rhythm, Forsyth’s music will bring a smile and make the body move.

The Alex Coke & Carl Michel Sextet is just so good. Their last Rosette show sold out, and we can’t wait to have them back. The virtuoso ensemble is woodwinds (Alex Coke), guitar (Carl Michel), concert harp (Elaine Barber), pedal steel (Bob Hoffnar), upright bass (James Suter) and vibes (Carolyn Trowbridge). ACG Originals presents Thinning of the Veil

  • Sat, Oct 25, 2025 4:30 PM Sun, Oct 26, 2025 4:30 PM
  • The Rosette @ Austin Classical Guitar (map)
  • Google Calendar ICS

Mexican classical guitarist Dieter Hennings Yeomans returns to Austin with a program inspired by the experience of the “thinning of the veil”—that liminal moment in autumn when the boundary between the natural and supernatural becomes palpable.

Joined by storyteller Janice Muniz, this concert is devoted to myth, folklore, and the rich cultural traditions of Día de los Muertos.
