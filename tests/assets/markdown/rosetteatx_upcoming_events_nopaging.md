# [Flamenco Night at The Rosette](/upcoming-events/flamenco-night-at-the-
rosette)

  * Friday, May 15, 2026
  * 8:00 PM 10:00 PM
  * The Rosette @ Austin Classical Guitar [(map)](http://maps.google.com?q=3908 Avenue B, Suite 116 Austin, TX 78751 United States)
  * [Google Calendar](http://www.google.com/calendar/event?action=TEMPLATE&text=Flamenco%20Night%20at%20The%20Rosette&dates=20260516T010000Z/20260516T030000Z&location=3908%20Avenue%20B%2C%20Suite%20116%2C%20Austin%2C%20TX%2078751%2C%20United%20States) [ICS](/upcoming-events/flamenco-night-at-the-rosette?format=ical)

“Poemas”

Poetry is the mirror of our souls - shared thoughts of love lost and found,
emotions expressed into words. In this show, dancers Drea La Flamenca and
Rocío Terremoto will collaborate to explore Spanish poetry—perhaps our own
original works, perhaps an ancient Caló poem from the Gypsy language. Flamenco
songs, or _letras_ , are poems in themselves, carrying deep emotion and
history.

We want to share our inspirations with you and create something truly special
just for The Rosette.

Musical accompaniment by our virtuosic Flamenco guitarist, José Manuel Tejeda.
Celia Corrales will join us once again with her passionate flamenco voice.
Tony Hampton will be our guest on cajón, alongside dancers Drea La Flamenca
and Rocío Terremoto.

Mexican classical guitarist Dieter Hennings Yeomans returns to Austin with a
program inspired by the experience of the “thinning of the veil”—that liminal
moment in autumn when the boundary between the natural and supernatural
becomes palpable.

and the rich cultural traditions of Día de los Muertos. It features music by
ACG Artistic Director Joe Williams, the legendary Mexican composer Manuel
María Ponce, and a newly commissioned piece by Austin-based composer Laura
Mercado-Wright.
